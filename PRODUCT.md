# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Founders and leaders of growing businesses — both established SMBs that need custom software to scale operations, and startups that need to take a product from idea to launch. They are evaluating a software partner: they want to know whether Thera understands their business, can be trusted with it, and works in a way that leads to real growth rather than just shipped code.

## Product Purpose

Thera (thera LLC) is a software agency that helps companies and businesses grow by building software with intention and purpose. The landing page exists to make a visitor trust Thera enough to book a call. Success = a booked call.

## Positioning

"Purpose-driven software for meaningful growth." Thera works alongside each client step by step — listen, analyze, build tailored solutions — instead of selling generic delivery. The name comes from the Greek _therízō_ ("to harvest") and the Terabyte: each project is a seed Thera helps grow and harvest.

## Product Principles

1. Grow together: the client is a partner in the process, not a ticket.
2. Trust through clarity: explain how the work happens; no hype.
3. Step by step: tailored solutions built incrementally with intention.
4. Purpose over output: software is measured by the growth it creates.
